Shop in the frontend

The shop forms run from the selection of items via shopping cart and customer data to the completion of an order.

  1. Check item, variant, quantity and displayed price.
  2. In the shopping cart, check all positions and shipping costs.
  3. Enter invoice and delivery data completely and select the offered payment and shipping method.
  4. Check the summary before sending the order binding.
An order is only deemed to be submitted when a clear closing or confirmation message is displayed.
