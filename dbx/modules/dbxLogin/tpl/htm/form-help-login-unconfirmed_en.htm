Send the confirmation link again

The password has been accepted, but the email address belonging to the account is not yet confirmed.

  1. Check whether the displayed email address belongs to your account.
  2. Select Send the confirmation link again. This creates a new, time-limited link; An older link may be unreasonable afterwards.
  3. Open the new email and confirm the account. Then you can register normally.
Only request a new link if you have access to the displayed mailbox.
