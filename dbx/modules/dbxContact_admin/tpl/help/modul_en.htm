What is this module for?

dbxContact_admin is the processing center for all contact requests. Here you can see open tickets, reply customers and keep an eye on the status.

What can you do with it?

  • Filter requests by status, priority or keyword
  • Read the full message history
  • Write public responses or internal notes
  • Adjust status and priority
  • Send answers optionally by e-mail
Tip: Internal notes are suitable for team meetings — the customer does not see them.
