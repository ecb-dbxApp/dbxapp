What is this module for?

dbxMenu builds the navigation bars of the application — the main menu for visitors and the admin menu for administrators.

What can you do with it?

  • Show menu items and submenus
  • Display appropriate entries depending on the role
  • Reach language, design and editor access via the bar
Note: The contents of the menus come from the CMS and the module configuration, dbxMenu bring it visibly to the screen.
