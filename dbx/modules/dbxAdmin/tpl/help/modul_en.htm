What is this module for?

dbxAdmin is the central administration of your dbXapp-installation. Here you keep an overview of the system and control important areas from a single source.

What can you do with it?

  • Manage, activate and grant rights to modules
  • Maintain users, groups and accesses
  • Maintain databases and content structures
  • View system messages, protocols and configuration
  • Connecting workflows and other subject modules
Tip: Start in the dashboard – where you can see at a glance what needs attention and jump directly into the appropriate area.
