What is this module for?

dbxContent Shows the content of your website to visitors — pages, text, images and structured areas that you maintain in the CMS.

What can you do with it?

  • View and view public pages and areas
  • Deliver multilingual content in the appropriate language
  • Represent linked media and embedded elements
Note: edit content in dbxContent_admin. This module is the stage — there your visitors will see the result.
