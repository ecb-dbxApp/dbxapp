What is this module for?

dbx is the core technical module of the application. It provides common services on which all other modules are built — for example, media, news and basic system functions.

What does that mean to you?

  • As a user, you usually do not call this module directly.
  • It ensures in the background that the platform works stable and uniformly.
  • Administrators use it indirectly through settings and other management modules.
In short: dbx It is the base, like the foundation of a house that is rarely seen, but without which nothing stands.
